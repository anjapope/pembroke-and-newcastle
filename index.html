Institutional Procedures

Procedure 14-C · Collection Designations · revised March 2009
Use an institutionally authorized collection line. Unconventional but defensible assignments require a brief selector note; clearly incompatible assignments require correction.
Procedure 8-B · Fund Accounting Codes · revised June 2012
Use a fund code authorized for the institution. Fund codes are accounting designations and do not create separate balances. Explain an unusual specialized assignment.
Procedure 11-A · Requisition Quantities · revised February 2016
Quantities must be positive whole numbers within catalog limits, with one line per catalog item and no more than one unit of a unique offering. Unit prices and recomputed totals must match the active catalog.
Procedure 22-D · Annual Allocation Review · revised January 2024
Only processed requisitions are committed. Obtain explicit acknowledgment at 80% cumulative annual use; qualifying one-time expenditures also require a brief justification.
Procedure 3-F · Submission and Resubmission · revised September 2018
Submit a complete requisition for review. Returned requisitions retain their identifier and prior submission record; corrections require explicit resubmission.
